# 1. PICO Framework – SomniSense

| Component | Description |
|---|---|
| **P – Population / Problem** | Adults sleeping in home/real-world environments, focusing on **sleep quality, sleep-disordered breathing, Obstructive Sleep Apnea (OSA), or AHI-related problems**. |
| **I – Intervention / Technique** | **Non-wearable sensing approaches**, including **audio/breathing sound/snoring, motion/accelerometer sensing, and active sonar** for sleep monitoring and respiratory-event detection. |
| **C – Comparison / Baseline** | **Polysomnography (PSG)** or other validated sleep-assessment methods used as the reference standard. |
| **O – Outcome / Target Metrics** | **AHI, sleep-quality indicators, sensitivity, specificity, accuracy, F1-score, false-alarm rate, and robustness** to ambient noise and movement artifacts. |

---

# 2. Inclusion Criteria – Tiêu chí chọn bài báo

| Code | Criterion | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **IC-Y** | Publication Year | Studies published from **2020 onward**, to ensure up-to-date evidence on sleep monitoring, OSA/AHI detection, and non-wearable sensing technologies. |
| **IC-P** | Research Task / Problem | Studies addressing **sleep monitoring, sleep-quality assessment, sleep-disordered breathing, OSA, or AHI detection in adults**. |
| **IC-I** | Technology / Technique | Studies using **audio/breathing sounds/snoring, motion/accelerometer sensing, or active sonar** for non-wearable sleep monitoring or respiratory-event detection. |
| **IC-C** | Comparison / Reference | Studies using **PSG or another validated sleep-assessment method** as a reference or comparison standard. |
| **IC-O** | Evaluation Outcomes | Studies reporting measurable outcomes such as **AHI, sensitivity, specificity, accuracy, F1-score, false-alarm rate, or robustness**. |
| **IC-E** | External Factors | Studies investigating factors that may affect **sleep quality or OSA**, such as alcohol, caffeine, noise, light exposure, stress, physical activity, or other relevant environmental/behavioral factors. |

---

# 3. Exclusion Criteria – Tiêu chí loại bài báo

| Code | Criterion | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **EC-Y** | Publication Year | Studies published **before 2020**, unless specifically required as a key reference by the research team. |
| **EC-P** | Unrelated Research Problem | Studies that do not address **sleep, sleep quality, sleep-disordered breathing, OSA, or AHI**. |
| **EC-I** | Incompatible Technology | Studies relying exclusively on **specialized wearable sensors** or technologies that cannot be reasonably applied to the non-wearable SomniSense system. |
| **EC-O** | Unrelated Outcomes | Studies focusing only on **stroke or cardiovascular disease without a direct connection to sleep, OSA, or AHI**. |
| **EC-E** | Insufficient Evidence | Studies without sufficient **experimental data, methodology, or quantitative results** to evaluate their findings. |
| **EC-D** | Duplicate / Irrelevant Data | **Duplicate studies**, irrelevant studies, or studies whose full text and methodological information cannot be sufficiently accessed or evaluated. |
