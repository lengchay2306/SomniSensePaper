# Inclusion and Exclusion Criteria

| Code | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| IC-L | The paper must be written in English.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| IC-T | Published in a reference source or journal, not a blog or thesis.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| IC-E | Contains at least one numerical result in a table or figure.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| EC-D | Duplicate of an existing paper.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| EC-A | Full text cannot be accessed.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| EC-S | Fewer than four pages, such as an abstract or poster.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| EC-N | No experiment, such as a vision paper or tutorial.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| IC-Y | Published in 2020 or later, following the release of GPT-3 in 2020.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| IC-P | Detects, classifies, or estimates sleep-related respiratory events (Sleep-Disordered Breathing, OSA, apnea, hypopnea, or AHI), snoring, or sleep restlessness, body movement, or sleep staging.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| IC-I | Uses a non-wearable, contactless, ambient, or consumer setup with AI, machine learning, or signal processing based on at least one signal stream:<br><br>1. **Audio / acoustic:** phone microphones, smart speakers, or ambient microphones recording snoring and breathing.<br>2. **Motion / accelerometer / vibration:** bed or mattress accelerometers, pressure mats, body vibration sensors (BCG/SCG), or contactless radar/sonar.<br>3. **Multimodal fusion:** a combination of audio and motion. |
| IC-C | Includes experimental validation against a reference: a clinical gold-standard label (PSG or HSAT) or practically labeled audio/motion data (ground truth).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| EC-O | Not related to measurement technology or sensing:<br><br>1. Evaluates sleep only with subjective questionnaires such as PSQI or ESS, without physiological devices or signals.<br>2. Measures daytime sports activity, such as steps or calories.<br>3. Focuses solely on surgery or medication without a measurement or monitoring solution.<br>4. Focuses solely on pathology or cognitive complications without measuring sleep.                                                                     |
| EC-W | Pure wearable device study: uses sensors in direct contact with the body as the primary signal source (smartwatch, smartband, smart ring, chest belt, EEG headset, etc.) without a non-wearable, smartphone, or ambient solution.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| EC-1 | Pediatrics: participants are children or infants under 18 years old.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| EC-2 | Animal or in-vitro study: experiments on animals or cell/tissue models in a laboratory.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
