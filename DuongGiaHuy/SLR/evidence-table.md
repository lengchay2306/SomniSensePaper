| **Acoustic Screening for Obstructive Sleep Apnea in Home Environments Based on Deep Neural Networks** *(Romero et al., 2022)* | Deep Neural Network (**DNN**) with a 30-s context window (10-s step) to capture temporal breathing patterns. Small model footprint (~6 million parameters). | 103 adults, overnight home recordings (>1,000 hours) synchronized with HSAT (SOMNOtouch RESP). Total of 157 nights. | Sensitivity (Se), Specificity (Sp), Area Under the ROC Curve (AUC), and estimated AHI. | **Moderate OSA Screening (AHI ≥ 15):**<br>- Sensitivity: 0.79<br>- Specificity: 0.80<br><br>**Severe OSA Screening (AHI ≥ 30):**<br>- Sensitivity: 0.78<br>- Specificity: 0.93<br><br>Fast execution on mobile CPU (iPhone SE 2020: 4s for 9.6h of audio). | Not specified | - Sensitive to room acoustics and environmental noise.<br>- Entropy-based voice activity detector may misclassify quiet/shallow breathing as silence.<br>- Lacks auxiliary physiological channels to monitor sleep architecture. |
| **Estimating Respiratory Effort from Nocturnal Breathing Sounds for Obstructive Sleep Apnoea Screening** *(Xu et al., 2025)* | **Latent-Space Fusion (LSF)** framework. Uses a BiLSTM encoder-decoder to predict respiratory effort directly from Mel-spectrogram sleep sounds, integrated with a 3-block CNN classifier. | 103 adults, 157 home recording nights (synchronized with abdominal effort signals from HSAT SOMNOtouch RESP). Average duration of 7 hours per night. | Concordance Correlation Coefficient (CCC), RMSE, MAE (for effort estimation); Sensitivity, Specificity, AUC (for OSA classification). | **Respiratory Effort Estimation:**<br>- CCC: 0.478 ± 0.133<br>- RMSE: 1.053 ± 0.123<br>- MAE: 0.793 ± 0.092<br><br>**OSA Screening (LSF vs Audio-only):**<br>Fusing estimated respiratory effort significantly improves Sensitivity and AUC at lower thresholds (AHI ≥ 5, 15). | Not specified (Corresponding author: *xxu97@sheffield.ac.uk*) | - Estimated respiratory effort has lower temporal resolution and accuracy than physical abdominal belts.<br>- Prone to phase misalignment between audio and physical chest wall effort.<br>- Diminishing added value at severe thresholds (AHI ≥ 30) where acoustic occlusion cues are already dominant. |
| **Evaluating Prediction Models of Sleep Apnea From Smartphone-Recorded Sleep Breathing Sounds** *(Cho et al., 2022)* | **Random Forest (RF)** machine learning model developed via the WEKA engine. Extracts 508 temporal and spectral acoustic features. | 423 clinic patients. Bedside smartphone (LG G3) recordings (1m from head) synchronized with overnight in-lab PSG. Split: 256 training, 167 testing. | Diagnostic Accuracy, Sensitivity, Specificity, AUC, Bland-Altman bias, and Correlation coefficient (r). | **Diagnostic Accuracy:**<br>- AHI ≥ 5: 88.2% (AUC: 0.90)<br>- AHI ≥ 15: 82.3% (AUC: 0.89)<br>- AHI ≥ 30: 81.7% (AUC: 0.90)<br><br>**AHI Prediction:**<br>Strong correlation (r = 0.78, RMSE = 14.73) using raw, unfiltered audio coupled with robust feature selection. | Not specified (built using WEKA engine) | - Data imbalance and skewed clinical distribution in the training cohort.<br>- Inability to distinguish awake state from actual sleep (total sleep time overestimation).<br>- All training data recorded in controlled clinical labs; requires further validation in home settings. |
| **In-Home Smartphone-Based Prediction of Obstructive Sleep Apnea in Conjunction With Level 2 Home Polysomnography** *(Han et al., 2024)* | Deep Neural Network (**DNN**). Model pre-trained on 1,018 lab PSG nights, 297 lab smartphone recording nights, and 22,500 home noise samples to optimize noise-robustness. | 101 adults tested in home environments. Concurrent iPhone 13 (iOS) and Galaxy S20 (Android) recordings placed 50-100cm from head, compared with Level 2 Home PSG. | Sensitivity, Specificity, Accuracy, PPV, NPV, Pearson correlation coefficient (r), Cohen's Kappa (κ). | **AHI Correlation with PSG:**<br>- iOS: r = 0.958<br>- Android: r = 0.953<br><br>**Binary Classification (iOS / Android):**<br>- AHI ≥ 5: Se = 92.6% / 92.2%; Sp = 84.3% / 84.0%; Acc = 88.6% / 88.1%<br>- AHI ≥ 15: Se = 90.9% / 90.0%; Sp = 94.4% / 94.4%; Acc = 93.3% / 93.1%<br>- AHI ≥ 30: Se = 93.3% / 92.9%; Sp = 94.4% / 94.3%; Acc = 94.3% / 94.1%<br><br>**4-Class Severity Accuracy:**<br>Accuracy reached 75.2% (Kappa: iOS = 0.636, Android = 0.627). | Not specified | - Cohort limited to Korean adults, restricting multi-ethnic generalizability.<br>- Lacks sleep posture and SpO2 physiological signals.<br>- Level 2 Home PSG might underestimate AHI compared to Level 1 in-lab PSG, which could lead the model to underpredict mild cases. |
| **Real-Time Detection of Sleep Apnea Based on Breathing Sounds and Prediction Reinforcement Using Home Noises Algorithm Development and Validation** *(Le et al., 2023)* | Sequential convolutional neural network modified from **SoundSleepNet** (14-epoch input, 10-epoch output of 30s segments) with Mel-spectrogram extraction and multi-epoch detection. RANSAC regressor for AHI. | Trained on 1,018 lab PSG audio files, 297 smartphone (LG G3) audios, and augmented with 22,500 Freesound noises. Independently tested on 150 smartphone overnight home recordings. | Epoch-level accuracy, Macro F1, Cohen's kappa, and overnight AHI Sensitivity, Specificity, AUC. | **Real-Time Epoch Classification (3 classes):**<br>- Accuracy: 86%<br>- Macro F1: 0.75<br>Successfully detects recovery breath/loud gasp at event endings.<br><br>**Overnight AHI prediction:**<br>- AHI ≥ 5: Se = 0.97, Sp = 0.89, AUC = 0.93<br>- AHI ≥ 15: Se = 0.85, Sp = 0.84, AUC = 0.85<br>- AHI ≥ 30: Se = 0.96, Sp = 0.91, AUC = 0.94. | Not specified | - Substantially weaker performance on hypopnea compared to apnea (hypopnea accuracy is only 51% due to 1:10 class imbalance and subtle signal profile).<br>- Needs integration with wearable SpO2 to improve hypopnea chracterization. |
| **ApneaDetector: Detecting Sleep Apnea with Smartwatches** *(Chen et al., 2021)* | **Random Forest (RF)** classifier with preprocessing algorithms: Total Variation (TV) filter for noise reduction and Augmented Dickey-Fuller (ADF) test for calibrating accelerometer drift. | Clinical study on 20 adults at Penn State Hershey Medical Center. Users wore Huawei Watch 2 running ApneaDetector app parallel to full-night lab PSG. Captured 1,018 OSA events. | Precision, Recall, F1-score (event-level); Intraclass Correlation Coefficient (ICC) and Bland-Altman bias (AHI and Total Sleep Time - TST). | **Event-Level Detection (Apnea/Hypopnea vs Normal):**<br>- Precision: 0.9674<br>- Recall: 0.9625<br>- F1-score: 0.9649<br><br>**AHI Diagnosis:**<br>- Strong linear correlation with PSG (ICC = 0.882).<br>- Total Sleep Time (TST) estimation error: MAE = 26.4 mins. | Not specified | - Cannot distinguish apnea types (OSA vs CSA vs Hypopnea) due to subtle, overlapping wrist motion signatures.<br>- Degraded performance if the watch is worn loosely or hand is placed far from torso.<br>- Sudden body movements trigger false respiratory spikes.<br>- Falsely counts quiet wakefulness as sleep. |
| **Proposition of a new, minimally-invasive, software smartphone device to predict sleep apnea and its severity** *(Frija et al., 2025)* | Sequential **Deep Learning** model integrated in the Apneal® mobile app (v0.1). Utilizes built-in microphone (raw audio, sound power, snore/breathing probability) and IMU sensors (3D acceleration, 3D posture, movement intensity). | Single-center, single-blind study. 46 adult patients suspected of severe OSA. iPhone 12 mini taped to patient's chest during overnight lab PSG. | Sensitivity, Positive Predictive Value (PPV), AUC-ROC, AUC-PR, Pearson correlation (r), and Intraclass correlation coefficient (ICC). | **Automated Deep Learning Detection:**<br>- **AHI > 15:** Se = 1.0, PPV = 0.90, AUC-ROC = 0.85, AUC-PR = 0.94.<br>- **AHI > 30:** Se = 0.95, PPV = 0.69, AUC-ROC = 0.95, AUC-PR = 0.93.<br><br>**AHI Correlation with PSG:**<br>Pearson r = 0.87 (automatic), r = 0.90 (manual scoring). | Not specified (proprietary commercial product of Apneal®) | - Small sample size (46 patients) at a single center.<br>- High-prevalence clinic cohort (skewed population), requiring broader community validation to assess false positive rates.<br>- Chest-taping configuration is less convenient than contactless bedside placement. |
| **Obstructive Sleep Apnea Detection Based on Sleep Sounds via Deep Learning** *(Wang et al., 2022)* | Deep Convolutional Neural Network (**OSAnet**) inspired by object detection models. Uses prior bounding boxes to match event labels and a "Detecting while Slicing" inference technique. | 135 snorers for model development (116 training, 19 validation). Independent testing on 59 new patients. Contactless recording using Sony PCM-D10 (1m from bed) parallel to lab PSG. | Event-level Precision, Sensitivity, F1-score; Patient-level Severity Classification Sensitivity, Specificity, Accuracy, Cohen's kappa, and AUC. | **Event-Level Detection:**<br>- Precision: 0.81<br>- Sensitivity: 0.78<br><br>**Severe OSA Screening (AHI ≥ 30):**<br>- Sensitivity: 95.6%<br>- Specificity: 91.6%<br>Estimated AHI strongly correlates with clinical PSG. | Not specified | - Selected high-risk cohort (habitual loud snorers), not representative of general population screening.<br>- High-quality professional audio recorder (Sony PCM-D10) used; needs calibration for low-cost consumer smartphone microphones.<br>- Pure acoustic model; lacks sleep architecture or posture monitoring. |
